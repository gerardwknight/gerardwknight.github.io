Dastardly Words Support

Help, feedback and bug reports

Found a bug, think a puzzle is wrong, or just want to say hello? Email dastardlywords@outlook.com. A real person reads every message.

It helps to include your device model, iOS version, and the puzzle number (shown on the puzzle screen).

Common questions

I bought the in-app purchase but it isn't showing. Open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Where is my progress saved? On your device. Deleting the game deletes your progress.
